Mission Briefing

The Galaxy Ring Project aims to create a continuous chain of colonized systems forming a ring around the galactic center.

Commanders, the Galaxy Ring Project calls upon all explorers, miners, traders, and fighters to participate in one of the most ambitious colonization efforts in human history.

Our goal is to establish outposts and settlements in a continuous ring pattern, creating safe corridors through the void and connecting distant regions to the inhabited bubble.

  • Explore uncharted systems and claim new worlds
  • Establish mining operations and supply chains
  • Defend expansion zones from hostile threats
  • Map optimal routes through dangerous sectors
  • Document anomalies and scientific discoveries

Command Database

Frequently asked questions from pilots considering joining the expedition.

What ship requirements are there? +
Any ship can contribute! Explorers may use Asp Explorers or Anacondas for long-range jumps, while miners and traders can operate closer to established outposts. Even a Sidewinder can scout nearby systems.
Do I need engineering? +
Engineered FSD is recommended for deep space sectors (50+ LY jump range), but many ring segments are accessible with stock ships. We have carriers that can ferry ships to distant staging points.
How do I report progress? +
Use the EDSM sync feature or submit system scans through our Discord bot. Command tracks all contributions and updates the galactic map in real-time.
Are there rewards? +
While there are no in-game rewards from Frontier, the project maintains internal rankings, badges, and recognition for top contributors. Your name will be recorded in the expedition logs.
What about Thargoid space? +
Several planned route segments pass near or through Thargoid-controlled regions. Combat wings are assigned to protect colonization fleets. Check the locked regions map before plotting your course.
